### Details of the major fixes

**V-U4-1.** Two new Lean lemmas were added to L5.6a. The L5.6 proof was rewritten so that steps 2-6
use only `collapse_sat_iff_image`. `collapse_sat_iff` (L5.6 item 2) is kept as the corollary
obtained by rewriting with `π[H] = J δ`, and L5.9b and L6.5 use it only after condensation.

```lean
theorem collapse_image_transitive (H : Set ZFSet.{u}) :
    ∀ x ∈ collapse H '' H, ∀ y ∈ x, y ∈ collapse H '' H
theorem collapse_sat_iff_image {C} (hC : IsLim C) {H} (hH : SigmaElem H (J C)) {n} (φ : SetFml n)
    (s : Fin n → ZFSet) (hs : ∀ i, s i ∈ H) :
    φ.Sat H s ↔ φ.Sat (collapse H '' H) (fun i => collapse H (s i))
```

The L5.6 rewrite also makes the following points explicit:
- the ordinal check for `ν'` in step 5;
- the reflection of `∃ h x HistOK` in step 5;
- the final clause (step 8), which was "the history argument of step 5 applied to ξ".

**V-U4-2.** `histOK_exists` gains the clause below, and `HistOK` gains the Δ₀ conjunct "the middle
argument is an ordinal". `ZFSet.IsOrdinal.toZFSet_rank_eq` turns an ordinal set into
