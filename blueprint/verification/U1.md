### V-01 (major, fixed): the transitivity proof of `Tree.cmp` is not inductive as written

**Draft text.** Item 4 was proved by mutual induction on `s`, carrying only `isLE`-transitivity.
The mixed cases `lt, eq` and `eq, lt` were justified by "the congruence of `eq` under
`Std.TransCmp`".

**Why it fails.** At the label level that congruence is available, because `c` is a given
`TransCmp`. At the column and forest levels (`cmpCols`, and the height component of `cmpCol`) it
is the property being proved.
- The induction hypothesis at the subterm `x` gives only `(cmpCol x z).isLE`.
- Consider first components `cmpCol x y = .lt` and `cmpCol y z = .eq`. If `cmpCol x z` were
  `.eq`, the tail comparison `cmpCols xs zs` would be needed. Nothing constrains it, because
  `xs` vs `ys` was never compared.
- Congruence at `x` cannot be derived from `isLE`-transitivity with first argument `x` alone.
  The derivation needs transitivity with first argument `y` or `z`, which the structural
  induction on `s` does not provide.

**Fix.** The induction now proves three statements simultaneously:
- (T1) `isLE`-transitivity;
- (T2) `lt` then `isLE` gives `lt`;
- (T3) `isLE` then `lt` gives `lt`.

It uses a `then`-composite lemma. The label components use `Std.TransCmp.isLE_trans`,
`Std.TransCmp.lt_of_lt_of_isLE` and `Std.TransCmp.lt_of_isLE_of_lt`; their signatures were
checked. The statement is unchanged and true.

**Evidence.** T1–T3 hold for `Tree.cmp compare` and for `keyCmp ∘ relKey 2` on 1,944,000 raw
triples, and on 54,000 near-equal triples, with 0 failures. The Lean docstring of
`Tree.cmp_isLE_trans` now names the strengthening.

### V-02 (minor, fixed): no bridge between `compare` and `keyCmp`/`Tree.cmp`

The spine instances do not set `compare`, so it is Mathlib's default `compareOfLessAndEq`.
- For `Key`, `compare x y = compareOfLessAndEq x y` holds by `rfl`.
- For `Forest` even that fails by `rfl`. The default uses the instance's own `toDecidableLT`,
  not the locked `instDecidableLtForest`.

The consequence for the trace facts:
- `trace_agree` (L2.5 (3)) states its sign hypothesis with `compare` in a generic linear order,
  so at `WithTop Key` in key systems.
- `sign_of_agreement` (L2.5c) concludes with `keyCmp`.
- The paper combines exactly these two (txt:246-249), and U2 uses the combination (txt:469-475,
  566-569). U1 gave no way to connect them.

**Fix.** Three new declarations, each with an informal statement and proof:
- `Forest.compare_eq_cmp` (D1.1 item 4);
- `Key.compare_eq_keyCmp` (D1.4 item 5);
- `Scan.sign_of_agreement_compare` (L2.5c). It states the `WithTop Key` form of the sign
  hypothesis, proved with `cmp_eq_compare`, `StrictMono.cmp_map_eq` and
  `WithTop.coe_strictMono`.

Each is tested in §2.
