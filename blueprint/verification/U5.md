### F1 — `isLim_add_omega` is false (major, fixed)

- **Draft:** `theorem isLim_add_omega {o} (h : 0 < o) : IsLim (o + ω) ∧ o < o + ω`.
- **Counterexample:** take `o = 1`. Then `1 + ω = ω` (Mathlib `Ordinal.one_add_omega0`), and
  `IsLim ω` needs `ω < ω`, which is false. The Lean proof of the counterexample is
  `scratch/blueprint/U5-verify/cex_isLim_add_omega.lean`; it typechecks.
- **Use:** L6.7, the `m+1` case, at `o = κ.ord`, which is a limit above `ω`. The use is sound;
  only the stated lemma was wrong. The scratch glue `glue_seed_config` passed `0 < κ.ord`.
- **Fix:**
  - the hypothesis is now `ω ≤ o`;
  - L6.7 step 2 derives `ω ≤ κ.ord` from `IsLim κ.ord`;
  - the N5.4 proof plan was updated;
  - `vglue_seed_config` re-checks the wiring with a real term.

### F2 — `stage_fit` missing hypothesis (major, fixed)

- **Draft:** `stage_fit` takes an arbitrary `σ`, with `hT` (realizable with fixed set
  `stageFixed ∨ top` at `σ`) and `htop`.
- **Problem:** the closed-root-owner case (its proof, step 3) builds `stageSetup` from the given
  realization `a₀` and needs S5.
  - The bank sits at the original values `α`, and its edges read pre-root representatives.
  - The setup reads those representatives through `baseVal`, that is through `a₀ (main x)`.
  - `hT` only gives `a₀ (main x) = σ (main x)`, which is arbitrary.

  So the bank graph, with mixed values, need not hold, and W cannot be applied.
  - Fixed values below `α R` *do* follow from `hT` and `htop`, through the budget atoms
    `lt (main x) top` and `lt (lab v) top`. So the capture case (step 2) was fine.
  - The exact equality `= α x` does not follow.
- **Fix:**
  - added `hσ : ∀ z, stageFixed … z → σ z = stageSigma α α b (b ∘ eν) (α R) z`;
  - the proof text now cites it.
- **Discharge:** in L8.6j it is StageInv clauses 4 and 5. `vglue_stage_step` composes
  `stage_transport`, `stage_fit` and `stage_restore` with a real term (no `sorry` in the glue).

### F3 — `stageSetup_clauses` missing hypothesis (major, fixed)

- **Fix:**

  - L8.6h step 3 discharges it from `hσ` and the fixed-value clause of `a₀`.

### F4 — `retAtoms` over-inclusive (minor, fixed)

- **Draft:** `retAtoms` keeps the structural atoms of `Γ(H)` inside `D` that involve `R` or `K`.
- **Problem:** this includes `elem A_prev R`, the ordinary pair entering `R` from the previous
  column of `R`'s forest. That is an old pair, not a retained one (txt:961-962: only the
  root-to-*next*-column pair uses `f`). Its `f`-image `elem A_prev h` is not an atom of
  `Γ(H[m+1])`.
  - Draft definition: 976 of 21,106 `f`-images are missing from `Γ(H[m+1])`, all of type
    `elem(pre, R)` (`classify_r22.py 30 1500`).
  - The forward direction (every structural atom of `H[m+1]` is old or an image), which is the
    one U5 uses, still held (31,263 cases at 24/400).
- **Fix:** `retAtoms` now keeps the atoms involving `K`. This loses no retained atom:
  - every edge of a retained entry has its slot in `K`;
  - `elem R A_next` has `A_next ∈ K`.

  With the fix, both directions hold: 576,318 forward and 111,093 converse cases, 0 failures
  (`test_u5.py 40 4000`). R22 now matches the paper's occurrence-map identity exactly, which is
  the form U3 is likely to prove.

### F5 — L8.4 unused (minor, fixed)

- **Problem:** `sStrict_all` used L8.8 at every `n`, so `sStrict_zero` (L8.4, a spine-named
  contract) was dead.
- Using L8.8 at `n = 0` is mathematically fine (only Case 1 can occur). It does, however,
  depend on U3's `firstDiff` returning `wholeEnds` for empty targets.
- **Fix:** D8.1 now splits on `n`: `0 ↦ sStrict_zero`, `k+1 ↦` L8.5, L8.6, L8.8. This is the
  paper's structure ("The base S0 is direct", txt:1211). It is checked as `vglue_sStrict_all`
  and `vglue_dCore_all`.

### F6 — block variables (minor, fixed)

- **Problem:** L8.3 step 5 says that `A`'s new values are `≤ c_A = blkMax A`. `blkMax` ranges
  over `owner` and `inner r` for `r ∈ K.roles`, while `hnew` only gives `w = inner r`. The same
  step occurs in L8.5f step 8.
- **Fix:** new lemma `posBlock_vars` (N5.6). Every variable of `posBlock K sel` is `host`,
  `owner`, `ord q`, `cur q`, or `inner r` with `r ∈ K.roles`. The proof plan follows the real
  body of `freeBlock` and the range of `resolveAux`.
- **Test:** 1,016,208 inner occurrences on the keys of standard expressions and 89,744 on random
  raw keys, 0 failures.
