### 3.2 U2 (owner U2): block form, stacks of admissible caps and of repair lists

**G011-D1** (`Closure.BlockForm`). `colH` with active index `eH` is in block form against `colE`
with active index `eE` when:
- `colH` ends at `eH`;
- `colH` agrees with `colE` before `eH`;
- the entries `eH … eE` of `colE` carry the label of `colH[eH]`.

That is: a deleted same-parent block followed by the active entry. `eH = eE` is the unmoved case.
```lean
def Closure.BlockForm (colH colE : Column) (eH eE : Nat) : Prop :=
  colH.length = eH + 1 ∧ eH ≤ eE ∧ eE < colE.length ∧ colH.take eH = colE.take eH ∧
    ∀ x, eH ≤ x → x ≤ eE → (colE[x]?).map Prod.fst = (colH[eH]?).map Prod.fst
```

**G011-L1** (`Closure.admCap_stack`; requested by U3 L4.2c steps 4(b3), 4(c)). The stopping
column `sd.col` of `w.H` has the column indices of `w.col`. For `q < sd.t`, the two stacks resolve
`q` to columns `oH`, `oE` of equal number, with `χ oH = oE`, and either:
- **closed:** neither contains its stopping column, and the two columns are equal; or
- **route:** both do, with active entries `eH`, `eE` (`χ` maps one to the other), in block form.

## 4. The proofs

### 4.1 U1 lemmas

**G011-T1(a).** Unfold `truncAfter = truncAfterBody` (spine §c.2, `truncAux`) and
`setHeight = setHeightBody` (`setAux`). By D1.2b `resolve_spec` / `OnStack`, `o` is either
- at a containing level `L` of `e.col.path`: `o.path = e.col.path.take L`, `o.col ≤ ck`; or
- at `e`'s own level: `o.path = e.col.path`, `o.col < e.col.col`.

`hno` excludes `o.col = ck` at a containing level (D1.2b `container_spec`). Hence `o.col < ck`.
Induction on `L`:
- at a level above `o`, both bodies descend into the containing entry only;
- at `o`'s level, `truncAux` keeps `cols.take ck` unchanged and `setAux` modifies only column
  `ck` (`List.modify`).

So `colAt o` is unchanged (D1.2b `forestAt_append`).

**G011-T1(b).** The same induction.
- At the level of `o` with step `(ck, u)`, `truncAux` gives
  `col.take u ++ [(q, truncAux rest … X)]`, and `setAux` modifies entry `u` only.
- `truncAux rest …` followed by `setAux rest …` is `(X.truncAfter e_rel).setHeight e_rel h` with
  `e_rel := e.relTo (⟨o,u⟩).heightPath`. This holds because `e_rel ∈ X.entryAddrs`
  (`forestAt_append`, `forestAt_heightPath`), so both bodies take their `entryAt`-guarded branch.
- For `o = e.col`, the last level gives `col.take (i+1)` with entry `i`'s height set to `h`.

**G011-T2.** Induction on `e.col.path`, unfolding `Tree.cmp`, `cmpCols`, `cmpCol` (locked
`Syntax.lean`) on `G := (X.truncAfter e).setHeight e h` against `X` (G011-T1(b) gives `G` at every
level of the path):
- earlier columns and earlier entries are equal, hence `.eq`;
- at a containing entry the labels are equal and the heights compare recursively;
- at `e` the labels are equal and `cmp h A_e ≠ .gt` (hypothesis);
- after the path entry `G` has nothing, and a proper prefix compares `.lt`.

Hence `Tree.cmp compare G X ≠ .gt`, i.e. `G ≤ X` (D1.1 `Forest.le_iff_cmp`).

### 4.2 U2 lemmas

**G011-L1.** Write:
- `K := litCap E w.col w.cut w.par`;
- `X_s := applyMoves K (w.moves.take s)`;
- `χ_s := (w.moves.take s).foldr (fun m y => m.mapRole m.bwdIdx y)` (so `w.origin = χ_M` off the

Prove, by induction on `s ≤ M = |w.moves|`:
- (I1) `stopData X_s = some sd_s` with `sd_s.col = c_s`;
- (I2) the conclusion of G011-L1 with `(X_s, c_s, χ_s)` in place of `(w.H, sd.col, w.origin)`.

At `s = M`, `X_M = w.H` and (I1) with `hsd` gives `sd.col = c_M`. The column-index clauses hold
because `mapPath` rewrites only entry indices (spine body; as in D3.1e item 5).

*Base `s = 0`.*
- (I1): if `w.col.path ≠ []`, D3.1e `litCap_spec` (last clause) applies. Its `CondI E` comes from
  `hE`. For its `CondV K` (U1 D1.3a `condV_iff_lt`):
  - every entry of `K` other than `⟨w.col, w.cut⟩` is an entry of `E` at the same address with
    the same parent (`litCap_spec`, first two clauses), so V of `E` applies;
  - the closing entry `(w.par, ∅)` is carried by the moves to the closing entry of `w.H`, which
    has the same parent and a column of the same number (`applyMoveBody`; `ColAddr.num` reads only
    column indices), so V of `w.H` (`w.inL`) applies.

  If `w.col.path = []`, there is no move (`MoveOK` needs a strict container), `w.H = K`, and
  unfolding `stopF`/`stopCols`/`stopCol` on `litCapAux` gives the stop.
- (I2): `χ_0 = id`, and `resolve` gives the same address on both sides.
  - Closed columns: `litCapAux` keeps `cols.take ci` at each level and `cols.take j` at the last,
    so these columns are equal.
  - Route columns at level `L`: `litCapAux` keeps `col.take e ++ [(q, …)]` with `e` the path's
    entry index. That is `BlockForm (·) (E's column) e e`.

*Step `s → s+1`.* By D3.1e `moves_violate` at `idx = s`, the move `m := w.moves[s]` satisfies:
- `m.col` is the container of `c_s` at some level `ℓ`, whose last entry `j = c_s.path[ℓ].2`
  contains `c_s`;
- `i < j`;
- entries `i … j` of `X_s`'s column carry one label `q`.

By `applyMoveBody` (spine), `X_{s+1} = modifyCol X_s m.col (take i ++ drop j)`, and
`drop j = [entry j]` since `j` is last.

(I1): by D1.2d `mem_routeCols_iff`, the route path of `X_{s+1}` is `X_s`'s with the step
`(m.col.col, j)` replaced by `(m.col.col, i)`. Indeed only `m.col` changed, and its new last entry
is the old entry `j` with its height. The forest reached is the same, so the last route column is
`c_{s+1}` and holds `sd_s`'s column. D1.6d `stopData_of_route` gives (I1).

(I2): for `q < sd.t`, `c_{s+1}.resolve 0 q` is the `mapPath`-image of `c_s.resolve 0 q`, because
`resolveAux` reads only column indices and copies the path prefix. By cases on the column `o_s` of
`X_s`:
- *closed:* `o_s ≠ m.col`. `modifyColAux` changes only `m.col`. If `o_s` lies inside entry `j` of
  `m.col`, its address moves from `(…, j, …)` to `(…, i, …)` with the same content; otherwise
  nothing changes. Hence `X_{s+1}.colAt o_{s+1} = X_s.colAt o_s`.
- *route, level `L ≠ ℓ`:*
  - for `L < ℓ` the address is unchanged and only the last entry's height changed, which contains
    `m.col`;
  - for `L > ℓ` the column moved with unchanged content.

  `BlockForm` reads only `take eH` and labels, so it carries over.
- *route, level `ℓ`* (`o_s = m.col`): the new column is `take i ++ [entry j]`, with
  `e_{s+1} = i`. From `BlockForm colX colE j eE` and the label `q` on `i … j`:
  - `take i` agrees with `E` (`i ≤ j`);
  - `E`'s labels on `[i, j)` equal `colX`'s (the `take j` clause), which are `q`;
  - on `[j, eE]` they equal `colX[j]`'s, which is `q`;
  - the new label at `i` is `q`.

  So `BlockForm newcol colE i eE`.
- *origin:* `χ_{s+1} = χ_s ∘ m.mapRole m.bwdIdx`, and `bwdIdx` maps `i ↦ j` at `m.col` and undoes
  `mapPath`. So `χ_{s+1}` maps `o_{s+1} ↦ o_E` and `⟨m.col, i⟩ ↦ χ_s ⟨m.col, j⟩`. Roles of the
  route are not the closing slot, so `w.origin = χ_M` on them.

`CondH E` and the interval fact are not needed: the block form is tracked against `E`'s columns
on `w.col`'s route, not against `E`'s columns at the relocated addresses.
